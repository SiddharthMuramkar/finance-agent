### The As-of date: Wednesday 31 July 2019, market close

- Coca-Cola's FY2018 10-K was filed 21 Feb 2019 [V]. DMart's full Annual Report 2018-19 reached BSE/NSE on 23 Jul 2019 [V]: the first date every note (leases, staff options, debt) is public.
- 31 Jul is the first clean month-end after that, and sits just before events that would leak: new US tariffs on China (1 Aug), India's rate cut to 5.40% (7 Aug), India's corporate tax cut (20 Sep), DMart's share issue (Feb 2020), COVID.
- The US Fed's first rate cut since 2008 came on 31 Jul itself; the closing prices include it. A hook for the central-bank Lesson.
- Rule: the latest annual report plus prices on the As-of date. Quarterly reports already out are public, so using them is no leak; the Course may ignore them.

### What happened next (the Reveal)

- **Coca-Cola**: revenue about 4.9% a year from the restated 2018 base; operating margin 27% in 2019–20, a low of 21% in 2024 (a one-off charge), 29% in 2025. Share price $52.63 → March 2020 low $36.27 → $98.09 on 18 Nov 2026; total return with dividends about 12.2% a year [D]. Events: Costa (Jan 2019), keeping the African bottler (May 2019), a lost tax case, a strong dollar taking 4–7 points off revenue a year in 2022–24.
- **DMart**: revenue about 19.3% a year FY19–FY26; operating margin 5.5–7.1%; COVID dip in FY21; stores 176 → 500 (Mar 2026). Share price ₹1,482.70 → peak ₹5,900 (Oct 2021) → ₹3,459 on 18 Nov 2026: about 12.3% a year in rupees, 7.6% in dollars (the rupee went from 68.81 to 94.24 per dollar). Events: tax cut to 25.17% (Sep 2019), a ₹4,098 crore share issue (Feb 2020), fast delivery apps from 2024, a new CEO.
- **The Reveal hook**: two very different stories, almost the same return in their own currencies; the currency then splits them.
