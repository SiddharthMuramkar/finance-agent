# Other sources for the library, and their licences

Research notes gathered on 2026-11-01. Prices, limits and terms change, so recheck before relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Summary

No single source covers all of finance. The strongest bulk-ingest core is OpenStax: Git-hosted source XML under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, covering economics, finance and accounting. MIT OCW, Open Yale and NPTEL add lectures. Permissive institutional text: Federal Reserve Board and New York Fed. Restrictive: the IMF bars systematic download without permission, FRED bars storing or caching without consent, the St. Louis Fed's education site bars database incorporation, SEBI wants a permission email, and Investopedia bars scraping, downloading and AI use, so it should be excluded. CORE Econ is NC-ND. Best chart data: BIS bulk CSV, World Bank API, IMF data, EDGAR, US Treasury, and for India RBI DBIE, data.gov.in and MoSPI. Main gaps: India-specific depth from cleanly licensed sources, Indian market prices and yields licensing, and tech-company finance for reading Zitron, which needs EDGAR filings plus accounting. No source addresses private RAG directly, so that legal question stays open. This is evidence, not legal advice.

## Options

### A: Open textbook and lecture spine

- **Description:** OpenStax Economics 3e, Finance 2e, Accounting Vols 1 and 2, Statistics via Git. Selected MIT OCW courses (14.01, 14.02, 15.401, 15.402, 15.433, 15.501, 18.S096). Open Yale ECON 251 and 252 transcripts.
- **Evidence:** Bulk route is official for OpenStax (under about 425 MB across the four measured repos, including media). OCW has per-course downloads and an explicit AI clause. Yale offers transcripts. All are CC non-commercial licences that fit private study.
- **Weakness:** Gaps: India almost absent; OpenStax is first-course level; several OCW finance courses date from 2003 to 2008; no current market commentary; no Damodaran-depth valuation unless the separate job's material is added; no tech-company finance. Yale and OCW must be fetched course by course.

### B: India-first library

- **Description:** RBI G-sec primer and reports, SEBI booklets and presentations, NPTEL finance and economics courses, Economic Survey, plus OpenStax Accounting. Charts from DBIE, data.gov.in and MoSPI.
- **Evidence:** RBI Bulletin and DBIE permit reproduction with acknowledgement. GODL is broad. NPTEL lists Corporate Finance, Financial Institutions and Markets, Money and Banking, Indian Economy and others.
- **Weakness:** Gaps: thin global markets; SEBI is retail level; Ind AS and Indian company filings not reviewed; Indian market price and yield licensing unresolved. Licensing friction: SEBI wants an emailed permission, RBI's site Disclaimer mentions caching, NPTEL labelling is inconsistent, and transcripts are unconfirmed.

### C: Institutional global macro layer

- **Description:** The Fed Explained, New York Fed content, ECB explainers, Bank of England explainers, BIS reports, OECD post-July-2024 reports, World Bank Open Knowledge Repository. Charts from BIS bulk CSV, IMF data, World Bank and US Treasury.
- **Evidence:** Fed Board is public domain unless marked. New York Fed allows automated access, storing and derivatives. BIS allows non-commercial redistribution. OECD and much World Bank output use CC BY. BIS and World Bank data were tested live for India.
- **Weakness:** Gaps: no ordered teaching path, little accounting or corporate finance, uneven depth, mostly short pieces or dense reports. IMF Back to Basics cannot be bulk-ingested without permission, and signed ECB papers need authorisation. The tutor's course map must supply the sequence.

### D: Reader-target layer for Zitron and Damodaran style reading

- **Description:** SEC EDGAR filings and XBRL for chosen tech firms, OpenStax Accounting, OCW 15.501 and 15.401, Yale ECON 251. Charts from EDGAR company facts, Shiller data and Treasury yields.
- **Evidence:** EDGAR is free with no key, has nightly bulk ZIPs and a 10 requests per second limit. OpenStax Accounting has an official Git source. These teach reading statements, which such analysis relies on (inference).
- **Weakness:** Gaps: macro and India; filings are raw and not teaching material; US filers only. Licensing of Zitron's own articles was not reviewed, and Shiller's data licence was not stated in what was read. Works only as an add-on to A.

### E: Smallest blended starter

- **Description:** A's OpenStax and Yale core, plus RBI G-sec primer, The Fed Explained, New York Fed content and EDGAR. Charts from DBIE, BIS, World Bank and IMF data. Request IMF and SEBI permission by email, and query FRED live only if consent is not obtained. Leave Investopedia as links.
- **Evidence:** Each named source has an explicit licence or reuse statement quoted in the findings, and each chart source has downloads or a tested API, except DBIE, whose SDMX access is unconfirmed. Covers economics, accounting, finance, bonds, India and global data at a starter level.
- **Weakness:** Gaps: valuation depends on the separate Damodaran job; derivatives and quantitative finance are light unless OCW 18.S096 or NPTEL is added; no Indian equity data; CORE's stronger real-world macro is left out pending the NC-ND question. Rigour beyond first-course level relies on Yale and OCW lectures.

## Findings

- OpenStax books (economics, finance, accounting). Covers Principles of Economics 3e (published Dec 14, 2022, web updated Jul 9, 2026), Principles of Finance 2e (published Jun 24, 2026) and Principles of Accounting Vols 1 and 2. Format: web, PDF, print. Licence quote: 'by OpenStax is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike License v4.0'. Accounting Vol 1 page states the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. These are first-course textbooks. Other URLs: https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-economics-3e, https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-financial-accounting Source: https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-finance-2e
- OpenStax bulk access: books are public Git repositories of structured source. Measured via GitHub API: osbooks-principles-finance 38.7 MB, 132 CNXML modules; osbooks-principles-economics-bundle 86.5 MB, 5 collections; osbooks-principles-accounting-bundle 264.4 MB, 2 volumes; osbooks-statistics 34.9 MB, licence field CC-BY-4.0. Sizes include media. The LICENSE files in the finance and economics repos read 'Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International'. Best bulk route found in this review. OpenStax Terms: 'You may only copy, modify, distribute and otherwise use the works in the Service in accordance with a license' (https://openstax.org/tos). Source: https://github.com/openstax/osbooks-principles-finance
- CORE Econ. Coverage: The Economy 2.0, Microeconomics 10 units (including lenders and borrowers) and Macroeconomics 8 units (including 'The financial sector: Debt, money, and financial markets' and 'Financial and environmental crises'). Format: web, print, Android app, EPUB, video transcripts, and a test bank of over 900 MCQs (https://www.core-econ.org/the-economy/). Licence quote: 'licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International License'. Also: 'You may not use all of our content, for instance by republishing it on your website or by creating a copy or a derivative website – reasonable usage policy applies.' Terms effective 28 April 2025. No bulk dump found; the page offers EPUB per volume and still says the Macro EPUB 'will be available in 2025'. Source: https://www.core-econ.org/terms-of-use/
- MIT OpenCourseWare. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. AI clause quote: 'OCW content may be used to train, develop, and improve artificial intelligence and machine learning models', subject to attribution, non-commercial use and share-alike. Coverage: the Economics filter shows 77 results (https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Economics). Pages confirmed: 14.01 and 14.02 (2023), 15.401 Finance Theory I (2008, graduate, Andrew Lo; covers fixed income, equities, CAPM, derivatives), 15.402 and 15.433 (2003), 15.501 accounting (2004), 18.S096 math of finance (2013), 15.S12 Blockchain and Money (2018). Format: lecture notes, videos, problem sets, exams, varying by course. Bulk: each course page has 'Download Course'; no whole-site download verified. Market examples in the 2003 to 2008 courses will be dated (inference from course dates). Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/privacy-and-terms-of-use/
- Open Yale Courses. Licence quote: 'Most of the lectures and course material within Open Yale Courses are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-Noncommercial-Share Alike 3.0 license'; third-party content is excluded. Fee-charging 'tutor services' are named as a violation, which matters only if the app is ever charged for. Coverage: ECON 251 Financial Theory (Geanakoplos, 26 lectures including yield curve arbitrage, callable bonds, mortgages, leverage cycle), ECON 252 Financial Markets (Shiller, 2008 and 2011; about 23 lectures in 2011), ECON 159 Game Theory (about 24). Format per https://oyc.yale.edu/courses: 'downloadable videos', audio-only and 'searchable transcripts of each lecture'. No bulk option found; per-lecture only. Source: https://oyc.yale.edu/terms
- IMF Back to Basics. The older index lists about 45 short articles in four sections: Big Picture, How Economies Function, Finance, Economics in Action (https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/fandd/basics/index-old.htm). New ones still appear in F&D issues, such as 'What Are Commodities?'. Format: HTML and PDF. Terms are the most restrictive of the free sources: 'free non-systematic downloading and/or printing of Content from its Sites by Users for personal, noncommercial usage only without any right to resell, redistribute, compile, or create derivative works'. Also: 'Permission is required to copy or download IMF Content in any systematic way', 'The IMF prohibits the bulk download of information by automated technology without explicit permission', and it does not permit 'the training of large language models (LLMs) without explicit permission'. Fair use is up to 1000 words or one-quarter of a source. Request via copyright@imf.org. Source: https://www.imf.org/en/About/copyright-and-terms
- IMF data, a CHART SOURCE with far looser terms than IMF text. Quote: 'You may download, extract, copy, create derivative works, publish, distribute, and use Data obtained from IMF Sites', with attribution and no distortion. Covers IFS, BOP, DOT, GFS, World Economic Outlook, commodity prices and exchange rates; some products carry third-party terms. Live test: the DataMapper API returned India real GDP growth for 2024 to 2026 (https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/v1/NGDP_RPCH/IND). data.imf.org replaces the legacy portal, retired effective November 5, 2025 (https://www.imf.org/en/Data). India-relevant. Source: https://www.imf.org/en/About/copyright-and-terms
- BIS publications. Quote: 'Users may download, display, print out, photocopy or redistribute any BIS Material for non-commercial purposes.' Reproducing in other publications is limited to extracts: 'not more than 400 words of text or two tables or graphs' and not over 10% of a publication. Unusually generous for non-commercial use among the institutions checked. Formats are mainly PDF and HTML per the site's publications; no bulk text download found. Total publication count not measured. Source: https://www.bis.org/terms_conditions.htm
- BIS statistics, a CHART SOURCE with official bulk download. Quote: 'You can download a full topic in CSV or SDMX format as a single (zipped) file.' Topics include locational and consolidated banking, debt securities, credit to the non-financial sector, debt service ratios, global liquidity, derivatives, property prices, consumer prices, exchange rates. Terms quote (https://data.bis.org/help/legal): 'The use of the statistics is unrestricted, provided that' BIS is cited and use is not misleading. SDMX API available. Live test: India central bank policy rate returned 5.5 for 2026-10-19. India-relevant, global markets strength. Source: https://data.bis.org/bulkdownload
- Federal Reserve Board. Licence quote: 'Unless otherwise indicated, information on Board's website is in the public domain and may be copied and distributed without permission. Please cite to the Board as the source of the information.' Primer: The Fed Explained, 11th edition (formerly Purposes & Functions), PDF whole or by chapter, last update Feb 26, 2026 (https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/pf.htm). No Board bulk text download checked. One of the cleanest licences found; US-specific. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm
- Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Terms (last updated 6/9/2023) grant a licence 'to use, copy, and distribute Content for your personal or business purposes', including to 'Access the Content, manually or through an automated process or device', 'Download, store, and use Content in any format or media' and 'Modify and create derivative works from the Content', with attribution and labelling of changes. No AI-specific clause was found in the full text. This is the most RAG-friendly institutional wording found. Its Fedpoints explainer page showed site maintenance when checked, so its size and coverage are unverified. Source: https://www.newyorkfed.org/privacy/termsofuse.html
- ECB and Bank of England primers. ECB: 'users of this website may make free use of the information obtained directly from it' if cited accurately, but signed documents such as Working Papers need 'explicit prior written authorisation' to republish. ECB has an Explainers section. Bank of England (https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/legal): 'download, display or print the Resources for personal use', and 'The Bank typically grants permission for non-commercial re-use of the Resources, particularly in an academic or education context'; its Database is under the UK Open Government Licence with some exchange-rate exclusions. Its explainers (https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/explainers) are short and aimed at general readers. Source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html
- St. Louis Fed education material. Federal Reserve Education (formerly reached via econlowdown.org, now FRE.org) lists high-school curricula and personal finance resources; Page One Economics is described as 'Personal finance and econ basics', so the level is below this learner's goal. Terms allow reproducing content 'in their entirety, for non-commercial, research or other educational purposes' but, without prior written permission, 'you must not ... frame or scrape any content' or 'incorporate any Website content in any other database or compilation'. Research articles: 'abstracts, synopses, excerpts, and other derivative works may be made only with prior written permission' (https://www.stlouisfed.org/about-us/legal-information). FRASER, a historical archive with API and OAI-PMH, has terms that do 'not permit use of any data mining, robots, scraping' (https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/terms-of-use). Source: https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/terms-of-service
- FRED, a CHART SOURCE with strict terms. Size: 'more than 800,000 economic data series from over 100 sources'. Allowed personally: 'Create an app using a subset of FRED data through the free API'. Without prior written consent you may not 'Store, cache, or archive any portion of the FRED Services or FRED Content' or 'incorporate any FRED Content in any database, compilation, archive, cache, or other medium'. Also barred: use 'in connection with the development or training of any ... machine learning'. API terms require the notice 'This product uses the FRED API but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis' (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/terms_of_use.html). API v2 fetches all series on a release, but bulk use still meets the storage bar. India series can be stale and third-party: INDCPIALLMINMEI last observation Mar 2025, marked copyrighted (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/INDCPIALLMINMEI). Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/
- RBI publications, INDIA-RELEVANT. Coverage per the publications page: Annual Report, Trend and Progress of Banking, Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy, Report on Currency and Finance, State Finances, plus working papers. Bond primer: 'Government Securities Market in India – A Primer' dated 01/04/2020, about 32 listed questions (https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=711). Licence signals conflict. The 2025 Bulletin notice says 'Reproduction is permitted provided an acknowledgment of the source is made' (https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Bulletin/PDFs/02ED22042025411BF425269F4FE38497C0EF89205D92.PDF), while the site Disclaimer says 'caching and links to, and the framing of this Web Site or any of the contents are prohibited' (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Disclaimer.aspx). Format: mainly PDF. No bulk offer found. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Publications.aspx
- RBI DBIE, a CHART SOURCE and INDIA-RELEVANT. Official portal disclaimer quote: 'Reproduction is permitted provided an acknowledgement of the primary source is made.' Seven subject areas: real, corporate, financial, financial market, external, public finance, socio-economic. FAQ says data 'can be viewed and downloaded without login credentials', with Excel, CSV and PDF downloads (https://dbieold.rbi.org.in/DBIE/doc/Frequently%20Asked%20Questions%20-%20DBIE.pdf). The menu has an SDMX Data Query. Avoid dbie.rbihub.in: it calls itself 'an experimental prototype' 'built just for testing and demo purposes only' and showed a 5.25% repo rate for Jul 2026, versus 5.50% on the official ticker and 5.5 at BIS for 2026-10-19. Source: https://data.rbi.org.in/DBIE/#/dbie/disclaimer
- SEBI investor education, INDIA-RELEVANT. Reading material includes Securities Market and Safe Investing, Financial Education Booklet, Financial Planning Guide, Introduction to Commodity Derivatives Market, presentations (REITs, InvITs, ETFs, derivatives, mutual funds) and MCQs (https://investor.sebi.gov.in/iematerial.html). Format: PDF. Sizes by HTTP header: Securities Market Booklet 27.9 MB, Financial Education Booklet 3.4 MB. Level is retail investor basics. Licence quote: 'Material featured on this Website may be reproduced free of charge after taking proper permission by sending a mail to us.' No bulk download found. The investor subdomain's own disclaimer has no copyright clause, so whether the sebi.gov.in policy governs it is an inference. Source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/website-policy.html
- Investopedia should be excluded from ingestion. Its legal page footer links its Terms of Service to People Inc. Terms (effective July 31, 2025) grant a licence 'solely for your personal non-commercial purposes' but ban using 'any manual or automated software, devices, or other processes (including but not limited to spiders, robots, scrapers, crawlers, avatars, data mining tools or the like) to "scrape," harvest, or download data', 'use any data from the Services for the development of any software program (including but not limited to training a machine learning or artificial intelligence (AI) system)'. Also: 'no part of the Services may be copied, reproduced, distributed, republished, downloaded'. robots.txt disallows ClaudeBot, GPTBot-style AI agents in part, and CCBot (https://www.investopedia.com/robots.txt). The Firecrawl CLI refused it: 'Investopedia is not enabled for this organization.' Linking out only is the workable use. Source: https://www.people.inc/brands-termsofservice
- World Bank, a CHART SOURCE and text source, INDIA-RELEVANT. Datasets quote: 'You may extract, download, and make copies of the data contained in the Datasets', and 'Unless specifically labeled otherwise, these Datasets are provided to you under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)'; some third-party indicators differ. Live test without a key: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/IND/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD returned India GDP for 2025. Open Knowledge Repository reports use mainly CC BY or CC BY-NC variants (https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/pages/terms-of-use), and that repository is a text source for development and macro. Source: https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets
- India government open data, CHART SOURCES and INDIA-RELEVANT. Government Open Data License quote: 'worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, adapt, publish ... translate, display, add value, and create derivative works (including products and services), for all lawful commercial and non-commercial purposes', with attribution; exemptions apply. MoSPI eSankhyiki (https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/) reports 'more than 4600+ datasets' and covers National Accounts, CPI, Index of Industrial Production and labour force surveys. MoSPI's own reuse terms were not found. Economic Survey PDFs are at https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/economicsurvey/, with terms not verified. Source: https://www.data.gov.in/Godl
- NPTEL, INDIA-RELEVANT video courses. Site reports '3200+ unique courses'. The catalogue has about 218 Management entries and 313 Humanities and Social Sciences entries. Finance and economics examples: Corporate Finance, Financial Institutions and Markets, Security Analysis and Portfolio Management, Quantitative Finance, International Finance, Financial Accounting, Econometric Modelling, Money and Banking, International Economics, Indian Economy. Licence labelling is inconsistent: the footer says 'Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike' next to 'CC BY - NC - SA' (https://nptel.ac.in/). Bulk: a free copy program needs a '20 TB' disk and is addressed to institutions and organisations (https://nptel.ac.in/harddisk). Transcript availability was not confirmed; course pages show a downloads tab. Source: https://nptel.ac.in/courses
- SEC EDGAR, a text and CHART SOURCE for company accounts; relevant for reading Zitron-style analysis of tech firms (relevance is my inference). Quote: 'Anyone can access and download this information for free'. Fair access: 'Current max request rate: 10 requests/second' and declare a user agent. APIs at data.sec.gov 'do not require any authentication or API keys'; XBRL covers 10-Q, 10-K, 8-K, 20-F and others, and 'a bulk ZIP file is available to download all the JSON structures' nightly (https://www.sec.gov/edgar/sec-api-documentation). US filers only, so not Indian accounts. Source: https://www.sec.gov/os/accessing-edgar-data
- US Treasury yields, a CHART SOURCE for bonds and yield curves. Daily Par Yield Curve, Bill, Long-Term and Real Yield Curve rates are offered as 'Download CSV' and an XML feed. Reuse terms were not checked on this page. Related market series: Shiller's monthly US stock price, earnings, dividends, rates and CAPE since January 1871 as ie_data.xls (https://shillerdata.com/), with licence not stated in what was read. Indian yield benchmarks from FBIL (https://www.fbil.org.in/) carried only an 'as is' disclaimer, no reuse licence found. Source: https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve
- Open Textbook Library, a gap filler for finance. Its finance subject lists roughly 25 titles across pages, counted approximately, with per-title licences shown, including CC BY, CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC-SA. Example: International Finance: Theory and Policy (CC BY-NC-SA). Formats and hosts vary by book, so each title needs its own check; no bulk download is offered in what was read. Further broad text sources: OECD content published from 1 July 2024 is mostly CC BY 4.0 (https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html). Wikimedia offers full dumps with text under CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL (https://dumps.wikimedia.org/legal.html), which is quality-variable reference material. Source: https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/subjects/finance
- Indian law background for a private library: Copyright Act 1957 section 52(1)(a) treats as non-infringing 'a fair dealing with any work, not being a computer programme, for the purposes of— (i) private or personal use, including research'. This is a statutory exception. Whether it covers storing whole works in a private RAG index, and whether it overrides site terms accepted as a contract (IMF, FRED, Investopedia), was not established. A separate job covers Damodaran and Buffett licensing, so this is noted only as general context, not legal advice. Source: https://copyright.gov.in/documents/copyrightrules1957.pdf

## Still unknown

- No source addresses private RAG directly. Terms mention 'training' of LLMs, 'compile', 'derivative works' or database incorporation, but whether chunking and embedding for one person falls under those words is an unresolved legal judgement, especially for CORE NC-ND, IMF, FRED and FRE.org.
- Whether IMF, SEBI, FRED or the St. Louis Fed would grant permission, and how fast. No requests were sent.
- Indian equity index and price data licensing: https://www.nseindia.com/terms-of-use returned a not-found page, and BSE was not checked. FBIL showed only a disclaimer.
- Whether DBIE's SDMX Data Query has a documented public API; a rendered page confirmed the menu entry only. Which DBIE series is the best G-sec yield curve was not determined.
- RBI: no site-wide reuse licence was found. Publication notices and the Disclaimer's caching language were not reconciled, and only the Bulletin, Financial Stability Report and DBIE notices were read.
- NPTEL: which licence actually applies, whether transcripts are downloadable per course, and whether individuals can use the hard-disk program.
- MIT OCW: no whole-site bulk route verified, and per-course ZIP sizes not measured. Open Yale: no bulk option stated and transcript file format not confirmed.
- CORE Econ: whether the Macro EPUB now exists, since the page still says 2025, and whether the test bank needs registration.
- BIS terms: read as excerpts, so the General section was not fully reviewed for AI or scraping language.
- Licences for US Treasury yield data, Shiller data and the Kenneth French Data Library were not found in what was read. Economic Survey and MoSPI reuse terms were also not found.
- api.mospi.gov.in documents only a CMS admin API on a private address, so a public MoSPI API is unconfirmed.
- Rough size not measured: Investopedia dictionary, St. Louis Fed education catalogue, BIS and RBI publication counts, ECB and Bank of England explainer counts, and New York Fed Fedpoints, whose page was in maintenance. OpenStax PDF page counts were also not measured.
- NISM workbooks: only a third-party-hosted copy turned up, so official access and terms were not verified and it is left out.
- FRED API rate limits were not verified from the pages read.
