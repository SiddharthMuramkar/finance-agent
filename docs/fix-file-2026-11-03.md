## The short version

1. **Choosing a lesson doesn't open it.** The top card changes, but the old lesson's chat and widget stay on screen until you find and press **Continue**. Then the new lesson starts *underneath* the old one.
2. **The Tutor carries one topic's conversation into the next.** One session can hold two or three topics. This is the "pre-existing memory" feeling.
3. **"Continue" is the only way forward, and nobody would know what it does.** It posts "Continue." into the chat as if you typed it. Remove it.
4. **The quiz is hard to find and breaks the page.** The Tutor says it's "in the panel beside the conversation", but it's actually folded away on the left. Opening it hides the chat and adds 3,000+ px of empty page.
5. **Widgets and the Tutor don't talk to each other.** The Tutor can't see your slider values, it asks you to set values the sliders can't reach, and your widget work is lost on reload.
6. **Everyone shares one learner.** One passcode means one profile, one progress record and one set of notes. A second person would land inside your lesson, and the Tutor would call them a student in Mumbai.
7. **Passed checks may not be saved.** Old sessions say "You passed Check 1 with 3 of 3", but the Course Map shows 0 of 2.

# Part 1 — The new-learner journey (target)

This replaces the Continue button. A new learner should never have to guess what to press.

### 1.1 Home (signed out)
- Keep the hero text.
- Replace the screenshot with a **real, working mini widget**: the annuity widget, see Part 5. It needs no sign-in and no AI, because code works out the numbers.
- One main button, **Start learning**, which goes to the passcode page. Keep "How it works".

### 1.2 Passcode page
- Heading "Enter your passcode", the field, an **Enter** button and a **← Back to home** link.
- A show/hide eye on the field.
- Wrong passcode: inline message under the field, "That passcode didn't work. Check it and try again." I didn't test a wrong passcode, so make sure this exists.

### 1.3 Welcome (first visit only)
Shown once, when this learner has no sessions yet. Three short panels with **Next** / **Skip**:
1. **How a lesson works.** The seven steps as a strip: Story → Concept → Explore → Your turn → Check → Reveal → Principle. One line underneath: "The Tutor talks on the left. Things you can move and try appear on the right."
2. **About you (optional).** The four questions from `/profile`: work, where you live, any loan, saving for. Include a **Skip** button.
3. **Your first lesson.** A card for "Percent, percentage points and basis points" with what you'll learn and a big **Start lesson** button.

### 1.4 Every lesson opens fresh
This applies however the lesson is started: Welcome, Course Map, Next topic, or Resume of a new topic.
- **A new, empty chat.** Nothing from any other topic.
- **A Lesson Start card at the top**, drawn instantly from course data with no AI wait:
  - Title and subject ("Numbers & time value").
  - **You'll learn:** 2–3 bullets.
  - **Before this:** prerequisites with status, e.g. "Percent… ✓ passed" or "Growth… — not passed yet · *Study it first*" (a link). If there are none: "Nothing — this is a starting lesson."
  - **You'll use:** the names of this lesson's widgets.
  - **About 15 minutes · 2 checks.**
  - **A 7-step progress strip** with the current step highlighted. This replaces the text "Teaching Step: your turn, for Check 1 of 2".
- **The Tutor's first message starts by itself** (the Story step), streaming within a second or two. No button.
- **The right panel is empty** ("Widgets for this lesson will appear here") until the Tutor opens one.

### 1.5 Moving through a lesson: "Next" chips instead of Continue
- Most of the time the Tutor ends a step with a question, and answering it moves you on.
- For people who'd rather not type, show 2–3 reply chips under the **latest** Tutor message only:
  - Always one that names the next step: **"Next: Concept →"**, **"Next: Your turn →"**, and so on.
  - Extras when they fit: "Explain that more simply", "Show me in a widget", "Give me an example from India".
- Clicking "Next: X" moves to step X. In the chat, show a small centred divider ("Step 2 of 7 · Concept"), **not** a grey bubble pretending the learner typed something.
- Typing still works at any time. After answering, the Tutor offers the "Next" chip again.
- The progress strip updates as you go. Finished steps can be clicked to scroll back to where they started.

### 1.6 Checks (quizzes)
- When the Check step starts, the right panel opens a new tab, **"Check 1 of 2"**, and selects it. The Tutor says "Your quiz is on the right →", which is now true.
- While the quiz is open:
  - Hide the "Next" chip.
  - Show a one-line note above the message box: "Quiz open: the Tutor will help with these questions after you submit."
- After **Submit answers**:
  - A result card appears in the chat: "3 of 3 — passed ✓", or "2 of 3 — let's look at question 2".
  - The Check fills in on the progress strip, the Course Map and Progress.
  - The "Next" chip comes back.

### 1.7 Finishing a lesson
A **Topic complete** card with:
- The principle in one or two lines.
- "Saved to your Notes ✓".
- **Next topic: Growth, compounding and logs →** and **Course Map** buttons.

### 1.8 Coming back
- "Resume: <topic>" on the home page (already there) reopens **that topic's own chat and widgets**, exactly as you left them.
- Switching topics never mixes chats. Each topic keeps its own thread.

**Done when (all of Part 1):** a learner can go from sign-in to finishing Check 1 using only obvious, named buttons, and the word "Continue" doesn't appear anywhere in the app.

# Part 2 — P0 bugs (these break the lesson for a new learner)

### B1 · Choosing a lesson doesn't open it
- **Where:** Course Map → **Study this Topic** → **Go ahead**. Also Course Map → **Back to the default path**.
- **Steps:** Be in topic A with some chat. Open the Course Map, choose topic B, and press Go ahead.
- **Now:**
  - You land on the workspace. The top card says topic B, but the chat is still topic A's and the right panel still shows topic A's widget.
  - Nothing moves until you press Continue.
  - Then topic B's first message appears **under** topic A's chat, and A's widget stays as a tab.
  - Reproduced both ways: Percent → Probability, and back.
- **Should:** Topic B opens as in Part 1.4: empty chat, start card, and the Tutor's opener starting by itself.
- **Fix:**
  - When a topic is chosen (`POST /api/jump`), close the current session.
  - Start a new one for the chosen topic, or reopen that topic's own existing thread (see B2).
  - The workspace shows exactly one session, and a session belongs to exactly one topic.
- **Done when:** going A → B → A, each topic shows only its own messages and widgets.

### B2 · One session holds several topics (the "memory" problem)
- **Where:** The Tutor's context, and Past Sessions.
- **Now:**
  - After a topic switch, the Tutor keeps going in the same conversation: "We've moved to a different Topic. You're now on Percent…".
  - Past Sessions shows sessions tagged with two or three topics: Session 18 (Percent + Growth + Zitron), and Sessions 19, 21, 22 and 25.
  - Old transcripts show switches mid-lesson: "Understood. We're switching to a new topic… The Sensex story stays where we left it", and "The compounding quiz is still waiting."
  - So the previous topic's conversation goes to the model along with the new topic.
- **Should:** One session = one topic. The model only sees this topic's messages plus the short learner summary.
- **Fix:** Key sessions by (learner, topic). Build the model's message history only from the current topic's session.
- **Done when:** in a fresh topic, asking the Tutor "what were we just talking about?" gets "we're just starting this topic", not the previous topic.

### B3 · Remove the Continue button
- **Where:** The card at the top of the workspace.
- **Now:**
  - It posts a fake learner message, "Continue.", into the chat.
  - It doesn't say what it continues to.
  - It stays clickable while a quiz is open.
  - It's what actually carries out a topic switch chosen on the Course Map (B1).
  - In one of your old sessions it was pressed four times in a row.
- **Should:** Gone. The start card, auto-start, "Next: <step>" chips and progress strip replace it (Part 1.4–1.6).
- **Done when:** no new "Continue." messages are created. Old ones in Past Sessions can show as a small "→ next step" divider.

### B4 · The quiz isn't where the Tutor says it is
- **Where:** The workspace at the Check step.
- **Now:**
  - The Tutor says: "The questions are in the panel beside the conversation, and you answer there."
  - The right panel still shows the old widget. The quiz is actually folded up on the **left**, in a bar labelled "Quiz Check open … Show".
  - A new learner looks right, finds no quiz, and is stuck.
- **Should:** The quiz appears in the right panel as the selected tab "Check 1 of 2" (Part 1.6), and the Tutor's words match.
- **Done when:** at the Check step, the quiz is visible without clicking anything.

### B5 · Opening the quiz breaks the page
- **Where:** The workspace, after clicking **Show** on the quiz bar.
- **Now:**
  - The quiz squeezes the conversation to about 24 px tall, so the chat effectively disappears.
  - The page gains more than 3,000 px of blank space below the message box.
- **Should:** The quiz scrolls inside its own panel, the chat keeps its height, and there's no blank page area.
- **Done when:** with the quiz open, the last Tutor message and the message box are both visible, and the page doesn't scroll into emptiness.

### B6 · Everyone shares one learner
- **Where:** The whole app.
- **Now:**
  - There's one passcode and no user identity.
  - The profile ("student, Mumbai, no loan, saving for nothing"), progress, current topic, notes and sessions are all global.
  - The Tutor reads this profile at the start of every session. It told me so: "What you shared in your profile: you're a student, you live in Mumbai…".
- **Should:** Each learner has their own everything. See Part 6.
- **Done when:** a second passcode signs in to a completely empty account and sees the Welcome screen.

### B7 · Passed checks may be getting lost
- **Where:** Course Map and Progress, compared with Past Sessions.
- **Now:**
  - Session 18 says "You passed Check 1 with 3 of 3" (Percent), but the Course Map shows Percent at **0 of 2**, back at "your turn, for Check 1 of 2".
  - Session 6 says "The quiz is passed: 3 of 3 correct, and code recorded it" (Present value), and Sessions 11 and 14 also report a passed quiz. Yet Present value shows **Not started, 0 of 2**.
- **If you reset the database on purpose, ignore this.** If not:
  - **Should:** a pass, once recorded, never disappears.
  - **Fix:** Check the code that saves a check result. Make sure a new Tutor version ("The Tutor was updated, so this is a new Session…") or a new session never rebuilds progress from scratch. Add a test: pass a check, start a new session, reload, and confirm the pass is still there.

# Part 3 — P1 bugs (confusing or wrong)

### B8 · Widgets can't reach their own numbers
- **Annuity widget:**
  - It starts at **3.33%**, but the slider moves in 0.25 steps. One click right gives 3.5%, one click left gives 3.25%, and you can never get back to 3.33%.
  - The lesson asks you to find the rate where the annuity is worth the same as the cash option. That answer is **about 3.38%**, which sits between steps, so you can only bracket it.
- **Percent widget:** The Tutor says the "fixed-deposit rate rises from 6% to 6.6% … You can set the sliders to 6 and 6.6 to check yourself", but the slider only offers 6.5 or 6.75.
- **Fix:**
  - Every slider gets a small number box next to it to type an exact value, plus −/+ buttons.
  - Rates use 0.01 steps, and default values must sit on a step.
  - Give the Tutor each widget's min, max and step, and tell it to use only numbers the widget can show.
- **Done when:** you can set exactly 3.33% and 3.38%, and every number the Tutor asks you to try can be set exactly.

### B9 · The Tutor can't see the widget
- **Now:**
  - Asked "What rates do I have set?", the Tutor said: "I can't see the widget's current state. When you move a slider, the widget updates in your browser and doesn't send anything back to me."
  - Yet it keeps saying "Tell me what you see" and "check it with the sliders".
- **Fix:**
  - Send the open widgets' current values and finished tasks with every chat message.
  - When all "Try this" tasks are ticked, show a button: **Tell the Tutor what I found →**. It sends the values.
- **Done when:** the Tutor can correctly say what the sliders are set to.

### B10 · Widget work is lost
- **Now:** Ticked tasks and slider positions reset when you reload, and when you switch to another widget tab and back.
- **Fix:** Save each widget's state with the session.
- **Done when:** values and ticks survive a reload and a tab switch.

### B11 · Duplicate widgets pile up
- **Now:**
  - Session 14 has four tabs that are really two widgets twice. "30-year Treasury yield before the draw" and "…through the claim date" draw the same chart. "What the annuity is worth today" and "Annuity present value versus the cash option" are the same widget.
  - Session 3 made eight widgets.
  - The tab row wraps onto three lines.
  - After a topic switch, the old topic's widget stays as a tab (LTCM inside the Percent lesson).
- **Fix:**
  - Each topic has a fixed set of authored widgets. The Tutor opens or updates those instead of creating near-copies.
  - Only the current topic's widgets appear.
  - Keep the tab row to one line: let it scroll, or use a dropdown.

### B12 · The same lesson gets a different, weaker widget
- **Now:**
  - The Percent lesson once showed "One rate move, two ways to describe it". The title promises two ways, but the widget shows a single number labelled **CAGR** ("Compound annual growth rate").
  - That's jargon in a beginner lesson, and the Tutor had to explain "the box uses our growth-rate calculator set to one year".
  - On first load that widget also flashed "1% / 1%" and "Working it out…".
  - Another session showed the much better "one rate change, said three ways": points, basis points and percent change.
- **Fix:** Always use the "three ways" widget for the Percent lesson (see B11).

### B13 · "Widget 1 →" chips mean nothing
- **Now:** The chips in the chat say "Widget 1 →" / "Widget 2 →". They're numbered per session, including leftovers from other topics.
- **Fix:** Use the widget's name, e.g. **"Open: What the annuity is worth today →"**.

### B14 · Developer messages shown to learners
- **Now:**
  - "The Tutor was updated, so this is a new Session, started from your saved progress." It appears **under** the newest reply.
  - "Under the hood: 2 steps, 28,668 tokens in, 1,669 out, $0.110, 21.3 s" appears under every reply, with a table of model, cache and cost.
- **Fix:**
  - Hide both from learners.
  - Put "Under the hood" behind a setting, e.g. "Show developer details", off by default.
  - If a session restarts, say "Picking up where you left off" at the top, or nothing at all.

### B15 · No sign that the Tutor is working
- **Now:** Replies take 4–21 seconds. For the first several seconds there's just empty space under your message.
- **Fix:**
  - Show "Tutor is writing…" immediately, and "Still working on it…" after about 8 seconds.
  - Keep the message box usable for drafting.

### B16 · Course Map is a 14,000 px wall
- **Now:**
  - 95 topics, and 94 of them have their own identical **Study this Topic** button.
  - The topic **titles** and the **Check 1 / Check 2** pills look clickable but aren't.
  - Every topic except Follow / Judge / Analyse sits under one heading, "Foundation" (72 topics), even though each has a subject tag (Financial statements, Bonds & credit…).
  - Almost every topic shows "Not yet passed: …", because topic 1 isn't passed.
- **Fix:**
  - Put **Recommended next** at the top as one big card.
  - Group topics by subject in collapsible sections, with your current one open.
  - Make the **whole topic row** clickable.
  - Show prerequisites as "Do first: Percent… (link)".
  - Hide the Check pills until a topic has been started.

### B17 · The prerequisite warning has no way to "fill the gap"
- **Now:** "…builds on Percent, percentage points and basis points, which you have not passed yet. You can fill the gap first, or go ahead anyway." The only buttons are **Go ahead** and **Not now**.
- **Fix:** Make the main button **Study Percent… first**, with "Go ahead anyway" as the second option.

### B18 · The quiz lock catches ordinary questions
- **Now:** With the quiz open, I asked "what is a basis point?" and the Tutor refused: "I can't answer quiz questions while the Check is open." That's fair for the quiz itself, but a new learner just sees a Tutor that won't help.
- **Fix:** Make the rule visible in the interface (the note above the message box in Part 1.6), rather than only through the Tutor refusing.

# Part 5 — The annuity widget (your screenshot), done properly

This is "What the annuity is worth today", from the home page picture and the Present value lesson.

**Controls**
- Slider from 0% to 10% in **0.01** steps, plus a number box to type the rate.
- Quick buttons: **0%**, **3.33% (30-yr Treasury, 24 Oct 2018)** and **Reset**.

**Chart**
- X-axis: payment years 2018 … 2047, labelled every 5 years.
- Y-axis: $ millions.
- Hover or tap a bar: "Payment in 2029: $X paid → worth $Y today".

**Readouts**
- Sum of payments: $1.537 billion.
- **Worth today at 3.33%: $884.1 million** (teal, your assumption).
- **Cash option: $877.8 million** (amber, sourced, 23 Oct 2018).
- A plain-words verdict line, worked out by code: "At 3.33%, the annuity is worth **$6.3M more** than the cash." It flips to "less" above the break-even.

**Comparison bars**
- The two bars at the bottom have no labels today.
- Put the name and value on each: "Annuity, worth today: $884.1M" and "Cash option: $877.8M".

**Break-even**
- Code works it out: about **3.38%**.
- Don't show it at first. When the learner gets within 0.02%, or presses "Show me" after trying, show: "At about 3.38%, the annuity and the cash are worth the same."

**Try this (ticks itself off, like the Percent widget)**
1. Set 0%. The value equals the advertised $1.537B.
2. Set 3.33%.
3. Find where the annuity equals the cash.

**Tutor link**
- Values and ticks go with each message (B9).
- **Tell the Tutor what I found →** appears when the tasks are done.

**Home page version**

### Rules for every widget
1. Every number a lesson mentions can be set exactly (B8).
2. The Tutor sees the widget (B9), and widget state is saved (B10).

# Part 6 — Getting ready for a second learner

**Simplest version that works**
- One passcode per person, e.g. an environment value like `PASSCODES="sid:xxxx,second:yyyy"`. The person's id goes into the signed login cookie.
- Add a `user_id` to sessions, progress, current topic, notes, profile and widget state. Every read and write filters by it.
- A `user_id` with no sessions sees the Welcome screen (Part 1.3).

**To test the new-learner flow yourself today**
- Add a test passcode that maps to a fresh `user_id`.
- Or add **Settings → Start over as a new learner**. It asks for confirmation and archives your current data rather than deleting it.

**Later**
- Proper accounts with email sign-in links, so nobody shares passcodes.

# Part 7 — Should the Tutor's text be dark blue?

**Recommendation: keep the paragraphs near-black, and use navy for accents.**

Why not colour all of it navy:
- Long reading text works best in the near-black you use now.
- Links are already blue, so they'd blend in.
- Your colours already mean something (teal = your assumption, amber = sourced). A navy body would add a third meaning.

What to do instead:
- Give each Tutor reply a small navy **Tutor** label, or a thin navy line on its left.
- Make **bold key terms** and step headings inside replies navy (#002366, your button colour).

That makes the Tutor's voice recognisable without a wall of blue. If you want to try full navy anyway, it's easily readable on your off-white background. Try it on one lesson and compare.
